# Operational Transparency & Live Progress Updates

- **Active Progress Communication**: Always provide clear, concise visible text updates in the conversation while working through user prompts.
- **Explain Actions in Real Time**: Clearly state what you are doing, inspecting, editing, or running before or during tool executions instead of running silent operations that collapse into "Thinking..." or "Editing files...".
- **Step-by-Step Milestones**: Break complex operations into clear visible steps, explaining the rationale, immediate outcomes, and next steps to the user.
